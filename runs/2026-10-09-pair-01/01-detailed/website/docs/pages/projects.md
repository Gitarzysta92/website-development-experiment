# Projects (IU-PROJECTS)
The two localized project records compare the problem, intended users/context, output, stage and next validation question. ProjectsPage uses the HOME-owned ProjectFeature public props and evidence tables; it does not invent product data or portfolio filters. The relationship section separates design activity from sensing validation. Assets are original conceptual SVGs with localized captions, not product evidence. The order is ArtiSense then AIFIELD in both languages.
Edit the records through the shared invalidation contract. Check PAGE_IDS=projects browser routes, native overview/evidence links and company context; forward pages become observable at CP-ARTISENSE/CP-AIFIELD. Copy and asset permissions remain unreviewed. No external service is required.
