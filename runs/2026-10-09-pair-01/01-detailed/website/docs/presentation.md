# Presentation (IU-FRAME)
PageFrame receives the public page view and semantic header/lead/body/aside/footer slots. It owns document metadata, main and the skip link. One consumer-supplied H1 is mandatory. Generic Button, Figure, EvidenceTable and ConceptVisual consume props and slots, never fetch product data. ConceptVisual is explanatory original vector artwork, not a measured plot or a product UI. Consumers own localized alternatives and captions. The asset register stays private.
colors.css is the unmodified supplied palette. global.css owns 1200/1440/68ch widths, 18px text, 48rem and 72rem breakpoints and safe states. Navigation's actual fit can require the larger shared breakpoint. Change these centrally and recheck the actual long Polish layouts. Header and project-aside become nonsticky in short screens. No remote fonts or raster hero downloads.
Private component routes are emitted only for COMPONENT_FIXTURES=1. They use real components and synthetic copy; ordinary and published builds omit them. Use browser checks at 320, 390, 844 and 1440 widths before reusing changes. No product or approval data belongs in generic UI.
