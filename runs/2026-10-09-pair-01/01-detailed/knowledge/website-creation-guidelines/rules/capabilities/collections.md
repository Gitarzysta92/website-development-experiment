# Collections, portfolios and galleries: rules

Apply these rules when the [collections, portfolios and galleries capability](../../capabilities/collections.md) is selected, alongside the relevant [baseline and technical rules](../README.md). The capability contract supplies its inputs, applicability and verification cases.

<a id="col-01"></a>

- **COL-01** — New published entries inherit the applicable listing/detail layout and navigation without manual changes to unrelated pages.

<a id="col-02"></a>

- **COL-02** — Adjacent navigation traverses the intended complete collection. Wrapping versus stopping at its ends is an explicit choice.

<a id="col-03"></a>

- **COL-03** — Zero/one-entry states do not produce broken or pointless controls. Ordering remains stable across translations when appropriate.

<a id="col-04"></a>

- **COL-04** — Missing facts are omitted; missing imagery uses a deliberate fallback. Do not guess technologies, customers or outcomes.

<a id="col-05"></a>

- **COL-05** — Gallery entries preserve captions/alternatives, and any deduplication retains useful supplied information.

<a id="col-06"></a>

- **COL-06** — A gallery supports appropriate keyboard controls, closing and focus return; a single image hides unnecessary navigation and no images omit the gallery.

<a id="col-07"></a>

- **COL-07** — Sticky summaries and mobile adjacent links obey the baseline fit, spacing, visibility and focus rules.

<a id="col-08"></a>

- **COL-08** — Project palettes may vary through scoped tokens while shared structure, focus and legibility remain consistent.
