# Purpose and content

Part of the [baseline rule set](README.md#baseline-rules). Apply each rule to the selected scope and record outcomes in the [acceptance report](../templates/acceptance-report.md); record justified exceptions in the [decision register](../templates/decision-register.md).

## Purpose and content structure

<a id="pur-01"></a>

- **PUR-01** — Identify the primary audience, its main needs and the intended outcome of a successful visit before approving the information architecture.

<a id="pur-02"></a>

- **PUR-02** — Give every page or page template a stated visitor purpose, authoritative content source and appropriate onward paths. Do not force a conversion CTA onto every utility page.

<a id="pur-03"></a>

- **PUR-03** — Connect related content through meaningful contextual links, not only global navigation.

<a id="pur-04"></a>

- **PUR-04** — Give repeated content stable identity independent of title, order or translated slug.

<a id="pur-05"></a>

- **PUR-05** — Define which source wins when supplied materials disagree. Mark unsupported claims and unresolved facts rather than inventing answers.

<a id="pur-06"></a>

- **PUR-06** — Record whether supplied copy may be preserved, edited, summarised, translated or rewritten. Layout work must not silently change that permission.

<a id="pur-07"></a>

- **PUR-07** — Identify illustrative scenarios, sample data, concepts and unfinished products accurately. Do not present them as verified customer outcomes.

<a id="pur-08"></a>

- **PUR-08** — Maintain shared content in one authoritative place; reused answers and company details must not drift between pages.
