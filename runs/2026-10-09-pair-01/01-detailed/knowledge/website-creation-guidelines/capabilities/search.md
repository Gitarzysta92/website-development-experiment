# Search and filtering

## Applicability

Visitors can search or filter a collection of records. Select this contract in the [project brief](../templates/project-brief.md); it does not require adding the feature to another project. Apply the [shared selection and recording conventions](README.md#using-a-capability).

Subjects: Functional behaviour; interaction and accessibility; visibility and discoverability; data, privacy and security.

## Inputs

searchable records/fields, topic vocabulary, language rules, aliases, sorting and indexability of result states.

## Rules

[SEARCH-01](../rules/capabilities/search.md#search-01), [SEARCH-02](../rules/capabilities/search.md#search-02), [SEARCH-03](../rules/capabilities/search.md#search-03), [SEARCH-04](../rules/capabilities/search.md#search-04), [SEARCH-05](../rules/capabilities/search.md#search-05), [SEARCH-06](../rules/capabilities/search.md#search-06).

These links are the authoritative definitions. Also apply relevant baseline and technical rules from the [rule catalogue](../rules/README.md).

## Verification

exact query, alternative vocabulary, diacritics, multiple terms, no results, combined filters, reset, direct link and service unavailability where applicable.

Record conditions, revision/environment, actual results and unresolved gaps in the [acceptance report](../templates/acceptance-report.md). Selecting the capability or implementing its interface does not establish a pass.
