# Persistent guided assistant

## Applicability

A guided assistant is included in the agreed scope. Select this contract in the [project brief](../templates/project-brief.md); it does not require adding the feature to another project. Apply the [shared selection and recording conventions](README.md#using-a-capability).

Subjects: Functional behaviour; interaction and accessibility; architecture and implementation; data, privacy and security; operations and lifecycle.

## Inputs

purpose and end condition, interaction model, allowed context, inference provider, persistence boundaries, artifacts, submission action and data policy.

These links are the authoritative definitions. Also apply relevant baseline and technical rules from the [rule catalogue](../rules/README.md).

## Verification

hide/reopen, navigate mid-response, reload, stale update, interrupted request, retry, completed artifact, repeated workflow, storage failure and real downstream delivery where enabled.

Record conditions, revision/environment, actual results and unresolved gaps in the [acceptance report](../templates/acceptance-report.md). Selecting the capability or implementing its interface does not establish a pass.
