# Multiple languages

## Applicability

The website provides more than one language. Select this contract in the [project brief](../templates/project-brief.md); it does not require adding the feature to another project. Apply the [shared selection and recording conventions](README.md#using-a-capability).

Subjects: Content and information architecture; functional behaviour; interaction and accessibility; visibility and discoverability.

## Inputs

supported/default languages, route strategy, translation authority, fallback policy and regional formatting.

## Rules

[LANG-01](../rules/capabilities/languages.md#lang-01), [LANG-02](../rules/capabilities/languages.md#lang-02), [LANG-03](../rules/capabilities/languages.md#lang-03), [LANG-04](../rules/capabilities/languages.md#lang-04), [LANG-05](../rules/capabilities/languages.md#lang-05), [LANG-06](../rules/capabilities/languages.md#lang-06).

These links are the authoritative definitions. Also apply relevant baseline and technical rules from the [rule catalogue](../rules/README.md).

## Verification

switch from a detail page, follow old URLs, inspect metadata, exercise long copy and missing translations, and verify the default-language route.

Record conditions, revision/environment, actual results and unresolved gaps in the [acceptance report](../templates/acceptance-report.md). Selecting the capability or implementing its interface does not establish a pass.
