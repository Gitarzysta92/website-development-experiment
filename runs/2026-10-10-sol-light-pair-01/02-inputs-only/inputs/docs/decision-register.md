## Decision Status

Statuses distinguish `confirmed requirement`, `accepted default`, `proposed`, `assumption`, `exception`, and `superseded`. No exceptions or accepted implementation defaults are recorded in the supplied DR-1.0 inputs.

Missing business facts, account access, content rights, and financial commitments remain with their responsible owners. An unanswered question does not establish an accepted choice, and a supplied document is not universally authoritative.
