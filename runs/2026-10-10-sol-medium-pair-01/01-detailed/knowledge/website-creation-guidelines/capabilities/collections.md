# Collections, portfolios and galleries

## Applicability

The website presents repeated entries, detail pages or image galleries. Select this contract in the [project brief](../templates/project-brief.md); it does not require adding the feature to another project. Apply the [shared selection and recording conventions](README.md#using-a-capability).

Subjects: Content and information architecture; visual design and layout; interaction and accessibility.

## Inputs

entry fields, stable ordering, categories, approved facts, imagery, captions, related records and adjacent-entry navigation behaviour.

## Rules

[COL-01](../rules/capabilities/collections.md#col-01), [COL-02](../rules/capabilities/collections.md#col-02), [COL-03](../rules/capabilities/collections.md#col-03), [COL-04](../rules/capabilities/collections.md#col-04), [COL-05](../rules/capabilities/collections.md#col-05), [COL-06](../rules/capabilities/collections.md#col-06), [COL-07](../rules/capabilities/collections.md#col-07), [COL-08](../rules/capabilities/collections.md#col-08).

These links are the authoritative definitions. Also apply relevant baseline and technical rules from the [rule catalogue](../rules/README.md).

## Verification

add a new entry, reorder input records, use a long title, inspect one/no image, inspect one/no project, open/close the gallery and check a short viewport.

Record conditions, revision/environment, actual results and unresolved gaps in the [acceptance report](../templates/acceptance-report.md). Selecting the capability or implementing its interface does not establish a pass.
