# Forms and enquiries

## Applicability

The website accepts and delivers visitor submissions. Select this contract in the [project brief](../templates/project-brief.md); it does not require adding the feature to another project. Apply the [shared selection and recording conventions](README.md#using-a-capability).

Subjects: Functional behaviour; interaction and accessibility; data, privacy and security; operations and lifecycle.

## Inputs

required/optional fields, validation, destination, responsible operator, notifications, retention, success definition and alternative channel.

## Rules

[FORM-01](../rules/capabilities/forms.md#form-01), [FORM-02](../rules/capabilities/forms.md#form-02), [FORM-03](../rules/capabilities/forms.md#form-03), [FORM-04](../rules/capabilities/forms.md#form-04), [FORM-05](../rules/capabilities/forms.md#form-05), [FORM-06](../rules/capabilities/forms.md#form-06), [FORM-07](../rules/capabilities/forms.md#form-07), [FORM-08](../rules/capabilities/forms.md#form-08).

These links are the authoritative definitions. Also apply relevant baseline and technical rules from the [rule catalogue](../rules/README.md).

## Verification

invalid input, successful receipt, duplicate click, uncertain response/retry, expired token where used, receiver outage, blocked tracking and preview isolation.

Record conditions, revision/environment, actual results and unresolved gaps in the [acceptance report](../templates/acceptance-report.md). Selecting the capability or implementing its interface does not establish a pass.
