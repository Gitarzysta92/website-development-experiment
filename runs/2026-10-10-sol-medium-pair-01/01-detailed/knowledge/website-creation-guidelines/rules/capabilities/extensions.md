# Accounts, commerce and booking: extension rules

These rules apply when the corresponding [extension capability](../../capabilities/extensions.md) is selected. They require a dedicated target-project contract before implementation; they are not complete account, commerce or booking specifications.

<a id="ext-01"></a>

- **EXT-01** — Accounts: specify authentication, authorisation, session lifecycle, recovery, data ownership and privileged action tests.

<a id="ext-02"></a>

- **EXT-02** — Commerce: specify inventory/pricing authority, payment states, duplicate protection, confirmation, refunds and reconciliation.

<a id="ext-03"></a>

- **EXT-03** — Booking: specify availability authority, time zones, concurrent reservation, confirmation, cancellation and rescheduling.
