Prepare an investor-focused Artifactory website with Polish and English content, substantial ArtiSense and AIFIELD project information, and a working enquiry path. Production publication has not been authorized by these materials.

Authoritative companion records are the [decision register](decision-register.md), version DR-1.0, and [sitemap](sitemap.md), version SM-0.3. The sitemap owns page purposes, hierarchy, localized routes, navigation, and page-level content requirements. This brief owns source permissions, objectives, capabilities, and operating inputs. The register owns consequential decisions and dependencies.

## Project Identity and Purpose

- **Business:** Artifactory. Working description for drafting: a company developing sensing technology and photonic engineering tools through ArtiSense and AIFIELD. Final company positioning and ownership/IP statements need the review in [DEC-010](decision-register.md#dec-010) and [DEC-011](decision-register.md#dec-011).
- **Primary audience and outcome:** investors assessing Artifactory as a company; a successful visit produces an informed investment enquiry. Confirmed in [DEC-001](decision-register.md#dec-001) and [DEC-006](decision-register.md#dec-006).
- **Secondary audience and outcomes:** technical and commercial partners proposing relevant collaboration; AIFIELD demo enquiries support these journeys.
- **Evidence:** the supplied project material and existing website are sources for drafting, with their dates, conditions, conflicts, and limits retained. They do not establish current company traction, validated medical performance, or an active funding round by themselves.
- **Claims to avoid:** unverified clinical/accuracy promises, concept renders presented as products, simulation comparisons presented as measurements on fabricated devices, exploratory relationships presented as committed customers, and a dated project funding proposal presented as the company raise.
- **Project owner:** the user coordinates the project inputs. Named company content, technical, privacy, and operating owners remain to be assigned through the decision register.

### Enquiry Contract

The form plus email channel is confirmed. The following field and behavior details are proposed inputs for [DEC-015](decision-register.md#dec-015), not a configured integration.

| Field | Proposed requirement | Values and use |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Reply email | Required | Reply destination; visible label and useful validation |
| Message | Required | Visitor's request; length and validation limits to be specified |
| Project context | Optional | ArtiSense, AIFIELD, or unspecified; contextual links can preselect it visibly |
| Name and organization | Optional | Help the operator understand and answer the enquiry |

Do not add document uploads, payment details, or mandatory phone collection. Keep project/type selections editable. Use the supplied public `info@artifactory.tech` as the candidate email fallback and verify its operator. Do not use personal or confidential content in query strings.

Show success only after confirmed acceptance by that receiver; a notification email is a separate outcome. Preserve input after failure, prevent duplicate clicks, bound waits, handle retry safely, and keep public email accessible. Test traffic must be distinguishable from real enquiries. The provider-specific contract, limits, retry semantics, retention, deletion, and credentials remain unresolved inputs.

## Brand Layout and Interaction Direction

The selected palette and usage rules live in S8; the principal colors are purple `#662483` and teal `#087F8C`, with predominantly white/mist surfaces and ink text. Reuse the existing semantic mapping rather than introducing a competing palette. Project-specific alternate palettes and an appearance switch are not selected.

Established visual references are WorkOS and THREESIXTY, selected in the earlier design-reference chat on 9 October 2026. The user requested comparison "not in the theme way": focus on composition, typography, hierarchy, and detailed product presentation while retaining the selected Artifactory palette. The [reference record and captured examples](../references/mockup-references.md) supply the existing board and four screenshots. They are external design references; custom Artifactory mockups, exact fonts, and component designs remain open.

Typography, layout geometry, spacing, component states, icon system, and motion are to be designed under [DEC-017](decision-register.md#dec-017). Proposed direction: clear company positioning, readable technical explanations, substantial authentic diagrams/screenshots, and visible maturity/evidence labels. Fonts must cover Polish characters and have known web-use rights. Respect reduced motion; avoid decoration that delays access or obscures content.

Global and project navigation, investment contact access, and the language switch follow the sitemap. Proposed state policy: the route owns language and current page; menu state is transient; preserve form input in page memory after errors; do not persist message contents in browser storage by default. Any additional persistence needs an explicit purpose and contract.
