# Structured content and CMS

## Applicability

Content is edited or published through a structured content model or CMS. Select this contract in the [project brief](../templates/project-brief.md); it does not require adding the feature to another project. Apply the [shared selection and recording conventions](README.md#using-a-capability).

Subjects: Content and information architecture; functional behaviour; operations and lifecycle.

## Inputs

content types and fields, relationships, editor/reviewer roles, editing permissions, source of truth and publication authority.

## Rules

[CMS-01](../rules/capabilities/cms.md#cms-01), [CMS-02](../rules/capabilities/cms.md#cms-02), [CMS-03](../rules/capabilities/cms.md#cms-03), [CMS-04](../rules/capabilities/cms.md#cms-04), [CMS-05](../rules/capabilities/cms.md#cms-05), [CMS-06](../rules/capabilities/cms.md#cms-06), [CMS-07](../rules/capabilities/cms.md#cms-07).

These links are the authoritative definitions. Also apply relevant baseline and technical rules from the [rule catalogue](../rules/README.md).

## Verification

create, edit, preview and publish a representative record; check draft isolation, links, role restrictions, re-import and rejection of incomplete or invalid releases.

Record conditions, revision/environment, actual results and unresolved gaps in the [acceptance report](../templates/acceptance-report.md). Selecting the capability or implementing its interface does not establish a pass.
