Confirmed website decisions remain in the [decision register](../docs/decision-register.md); page content requirements remain in the [sitemap](../docs/sitemap.md). This document supplies background, not additional pages or features.

## Artifactory

The website presents Artifactory as the company behind ArtiSense and AIFIELD. Its primary audience is investors assessing the company, with partners secondary. English is the default language, with equivalent Polish content. The investor story should connect the two projects through an approved company proposition, their actual stages, and the next milestones.

Working positioning for drafting: sensing technology and tools for designing photonic systems. This is a useful organizing description; exact company/IP ownership, commercial positioning, traction, and funding figures still need confirmation. The user selected company-level investment scope. A historical project funding proposal must not become the company's current ask by inference.

Other research and the wider ecosystem are outside this website's launch scope. Current team roles, partner relationships, legal/contact details, and public materials should be reviewed by the responsible company owner before they become final copy.

## ArtiSense

ArtiSense is presented in the reviewed material as a proposed miniaturized chemical-sensing system using photonics and signal interpretation. Potential non-invasive measurement and wearable form factors are part of the intended direction. They must retain their concept or research status wherever the underlying capability has not been demonstrated.

The existing module description discusses visible/infrared light, optical response and reference comparison, and interpretation of spectra. It also describes a proposed reprogrammable analog neuromorphic processor, software support, and connectivity. These are components of the described architecture, not evidence that every component is implemented or validated.

Photonic glucose sensing is the substantial example in the supplied research. The background describes optical feasibility work and chip design. That does not establish a fabricated device, validated glucose accuracy, or clinical outcomes. The earlier homepage's stronger health/performance wording needs reconciliation with the more qualified project material before use.

The proposed project branch separates:

- **Overview:** problem, intended users, system summary, current stage, evidence, next milestone.
- **Technology:** sensing and processing architecture, calibration assumptions, component status, integration constraints.
- **Applications:** approved target uses, context, form factor, requirements, and gaps to intended use.
- **Research and Validation:** dated work, methods, results, limitations, open questions, and next experiments.

The planned ArtiSense analog interpretation processor is a different subject from AIFIELD's neural optical-field research. Do not describe them as the same technology.

## AIFIELD

The September 2026 material presents AIFIELD as photonic CAD and simulation tooling with a separate research direction in neural field prediction. It describes design/layer/process handling and mode, circuit, field, and thermal simulation work. Capability statements must specify what was demonstrated at that date and what has since changed.

The demonstration material includes internal comparisons and a photonic glucose chip-design example. These support a discussion of engineering workflow and simulation evidence. They do not establish measured performance on fabricated chips or validated sensing outcomes. Published benchmark summaries should include their reference, test conditions, successes and misses, and date.

Command-line and Model Context Protocol workflows explore agent-assisted engineering. The described workflow includes proposals and human application/review, with partial automation. It does not justify a claim of autonomous end-to-end design. The local-solver boundary and any externally processed assistant information must be explained accurately before confidentiality claims are made.

Neural field prediction is research. The reviewed results have conditions, including supplied boundary information; questions around from-scratch prediction, larger stitched domains, and practical speed advantage remain relevant limitations. Keep this research distinct from usable classical-solver functions.

The proposed project branch separates:

- **Overview:** intended users, workflow, current capabilities, research status, access, next milestones.
- **Design and Simulation:** engineering tasks, supported inputs/outputs, interface examples, classical solvers, constraints.
- **AI Workflows:** available actions, example workflow, human responsibilities, local/external processing.
- **Neural Field Research:** experiment methods, conditions, findings, limits, and next research milestones.
- **Examples and Validation:** worked designs, classical comparisons, benchmark scope, and result provenance.

Commercial model, current users/revenue, fabrication arrangements, partner commitments, and funding information need current confirmation. Exploratory discussions or a prospective letter of intent must not be presented as a committed commercial relationship.

## Relationship Between the Projects

AIFIELD's photonic design workflow can be explained through the sensing-design example associated with ArtiSense. Present the design activity and its outputs on AIFIELD pages; present the measurement objective and validation needs on ArtiSense pages. Progress in one does not automatically validate the other.
