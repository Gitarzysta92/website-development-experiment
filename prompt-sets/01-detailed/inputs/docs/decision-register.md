## Decision Handling

Stable statuses follow the guideline template: `confirmed requirement`, `accepted default`, `proposed`, `assumption`, `exception`, and `superseded`. No exceptions or accepted implementation defaults are recorded in DR-1.0. A library reference selects applicable requirements; it is not evidence of compliance.

The executor can resolve routine design and engineering proposals within EP-1.0 and record the outcome here. Missing business facts, account access, content rights, and financial commitments remain with their responsible owners. Do not manufacture an answer from silence or treat a supplied document as universally authoritative.

When a decision affects a compiled contract, update the affected units and plan, validate the complete replacement package, and record autonomous acceptance within the supplied authority before affected execution. Ordinary defects remain within their current contracts. Keep earlier decisions and package references when superseding them.
