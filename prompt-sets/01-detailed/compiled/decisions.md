The executor may implement the compiled decisions, choose bounded layout/copy details, repair defects, and evaluate each checkpoint without asking for routine approval. Continue through all eligible units. A failed check requires a repair or a truthful unresolved result, not an invented pass or weakened criterion.

For a material change to scope, public interfaces, structural ownership, dependencies, selected tools, permissions, or acceptance criteria: preserve the current package, record the reason, revise affected units and the plan, revalidate the complete replacement and its shared consumers, then record autonomous acceptance within existing authority before affected work resumes. Keep prior evidence bound to its original implementation and conditions. A compatible tool patch still needs a recorded lock change and affected verification; a major switch requires package revision. Do not compile the remaining units gradually during execution.

The executor cannot approve business facts or asset rights on another person's behalf, silently drop a project/language/contact channel, add deferred capabilities, incur paid commitments, or grant publication/external-send authority. Missing prerequisites are recorded with the blocked action, provider, and resumption condition; finish independent work. Request intervention only when no authorized path can resolve a blocker that prevents required local completion.

The initial plan and all unit contracts are accepted for this stated local scope after compilation checks. At execution start, compare the actual baseline and package hashes to that acceptance. Ordinary environment setup and previously declared outputs are not reasons to request operator acceptance again.
